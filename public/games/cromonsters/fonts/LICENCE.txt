Estate Ledger font

The Estate Ledger glyph drawings in src/art/font/letters.ts and symbols.ts,
the font-generation code under src/art/font/, and the generated
public/fonts/estate-ledger.ttf are original work authored for this project.
No glyph outlines, bitmap glyphs, or font files were imported from a
third-party typeface. The generator implements the public OpenType format.
